# Fund Edition — your AI investment team

Fund Edition aims to make active investing more accessible, less expensive and easier to understand. It gives everyday investors a team they can direct, follow and question, with two ways to begin.

## Build your own AI fund

Start with Maya, an AI fund manager. Tell her what you want to invest in, the kind of strategy you want to try and the risks you care about. Develop the mandate together, review the proposed team and hire specialists. Give a researcher a sector to follow, ask a risk analyst to challenge concentration, or change someone's assignment as your thinking develops.

The office makes the team visible. Maya stays the main point of contact, with individual colleagues available for specialist questions. Users can see who is responsible for which work, follow the fund's activity and ask the manager to explain a decision.

The value continues after the first conversation. The product vision is an AI team on duty around the clock, following market developments and relevant news while its owner is away. The current US-equity demo supports scheduled background observations, a saved activity journal and follow-up explanations. Its schedule includes a morning news check and market-hours observations at supported 30-, 60- or 120-minute intervals. It can finish a background task after the browser closes. Order execution remains subject to market hours, usable data and the user's authorization.

Start with paper capital and see how the team behaves over time. Return to review positions, gains and losses, new research and the reasons for a trade or a decision to wait. The commercial model is a membership for continued AI-team access, monitoring and discussion after the paper trial. Access is priced at US$9.99 for a month or US$99.99 for a year. The current personal-PayPal purchase path provides a fixed term of access.

## Choose an existing AI fund

This second entrance serves users who want an existing investment approach. It contains two distinct collections.

### Popular active funds

AI teams reconstruct publicly documented investment strategies using disclosed holdings, stated investment methods and historical information. The catalogue includes research based on ARKK and Vanguard Wellington. Users can inspect the strategy, underlying evidence, available historical studies and the roles responsible for decisions before trying the supported paper-investment flows.

The business opportunity is to reduce the cost of delivering an active strategy. Many users can share a strategy's core AI research and analysis. Instead of duplicating the whole research process for each investor, the service can spread that cost across a larger user base. The goal is very low management fees while maintaining independent account balances, permissions and execution checks.

The economic advantage is a design and pricing hypothesis that must be checked against actual research, data, model and operating costs. Individual conversations and account-specific processing still have costs. Each strategy's fees and underlying expenses need their own calculation.

### Investor-inspired funds

Users can also choose teams shaped by the public investment principles of people such as Warren Buffett, Ray Dalio and Cathie Wood. This gives a familiar starting point for exploring different approaches to valuation, diversification and growth.

The intended experience includes an identifiable team, a documented method, inspectable decisions and a manager who can explain its reasoning. Access to ongoing AI-team services can fit within the membership model. These are Fund Edition's independent interpretations of public methods.

## One portfolio, with an explanation behind each decision

My Portfolio brings self-built funds and selected catalogue strategies into one simulated account. A user can run several personal strategies and allocate paper capital to other available approaches.

The product's advantages belong together. Natural-language setup reduces the work of configuring a team. Background monitoring gives the team something useful to do between visits. A visible division of responsibilities makes its operation understandable. Decision records let the user ask what changed and why the team acted. Shared strategy research offers a route to lower delivery costs.

HarnessRouter powers the product's AI conversations. The current demo uses paper money. Broker integration and low-fee live investing are planned. Future brokerage partnerships may add a revenue channel once their service, permissions and fee terms are established.
